The Zephyr Valley Water Project

The Zephyr Valley Water Project is a small community initiative that installed 42 rainwater cisterns across the valley in 2025. Its organizers argue that decentralized storage is cheaper than a new reservoir, that it keeps water rights in the hands of local households, and that it reduces flooding during the spring melt.

Critics respond that cisterns run dry during long droughts and that maintenance costs fall unevenly on older residents. The project's coordinator, Mirela Okafor, says a shared maintenance fund will address this.
